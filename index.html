      Paste a video link from another site. It opens for your partner too, and stays in sync.
      What links work?
      ✅ Direct video or audio files (address ends in .mp4, .webm, .mov, .mp3…)
      ✅ Stream links (address ends in .m3u8)
      ✅ Vimeo videos
      ❌ Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+/Hotstar, JioCinema, SonyLIV and similar: they lock their videos (DRM), so no other site can play them
      ❌ Ordinary web pages with a video somewhere on them: you need the video's own address, not the page's
      🖥 Site with its own player?
      Open the site in another tab and start the video there. Then tap Share a tab and pick that tab. Your partner watches it live here, with sound. You control play and pause on the site.
      Good to know
      • Sharing works from a computer (Chrome or Edge are best). Phones can watch but can't share.
      • For sound, pick a Chrome tab (not a window) and keep "Also share tab audio" ticked.
      • Netflix, Prime Video, Hotstar and other DRM sites show a black screen when shared. That's their protection.
      • The picture goes straight between your two devices. Nothing is recorded or stored.
